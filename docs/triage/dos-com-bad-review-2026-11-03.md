The 40 snapshot artifacts include DOS programs, resources, translations, and other binary data. Cleave and Atom were run with cache disabled. Binary inspection and offline decoding do not execute samples.

`6cbbaea17fd2` remains bad. Its initial code asks BIOS for the first hard disk's geometry, then loops over heads and cylinders issuing INT 13h sector writes. The new `dos-hard-disk-geometry-multihead-overwrite` finding is hostile in both scanners. Changing the disk-write interrupt clears it. Atom's model probability was approximately 97.2%; full validation passed.

`7c7df57b8aa6` and `f13419628957` remain bad. Their entry jumps lead to position-independent XOR word decoders. The new `dos-word-xor-com-search-and-write-infector` combines the entry jump, decoder, and encoded search/open evidence; changing the encoded wildcard clears it. This limitation is documented rather than addressed with an engine change.
