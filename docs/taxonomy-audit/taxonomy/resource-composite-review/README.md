# Resource composite review

Twenty-six composites moved from the legacy resource-staging file. Their match
conditions, scopes, confidence, exclusions and downgrades were preserved; local
and external references were retargeted. Eleven resource-name atoms and five
composites that depend on them remain for the next review.

Resource access, allocation-shaped instructions and API imports do not alone
establish that a payload was decrypted, mapped or invoked. Those combinations
now describe their evidence as suspicious PE or graphics profiles. The linked
EFS setup-state damage and the API-hashed injection combination remain with
their respective impact and execution objectives. Resource, process and command
combinations that support a likely launch retain execution-oriented placement;
the descriptions avoid claiming a proven binding between independent legs.
The Delphi capture combination retains the likely surveillance objective while
its individual graphics, hook and network profiles remain separately visible.
