# Zyra VPN stage review

The 77-rule supply-chain branch was divided into product identity, installer, service, and generic observations. Zyra/ShieldGuard product fields and client identity now live with the VPN application; installer and service clues live with their respective loader and backdoor families. Generic method names, signer subject, endpoint paths, tool paths and co-occurrences have their own subjects. Product-specific fixed indicators no longer have an arbitrary maximum file-size limit.

The [MalwareBazaar sample report](https://bazaar.abuse.ch/sample/2b12094f3fea6829708fb1468577c733667604e88acc0354090652a0c950113d/) describes a suspicious installer and polling service, and presents traffic interception or residential proxying as an inference from the client configuration. A shared Shadowsocks credential and standard VPN tooling do not by themselves establish interception or a proxy botnet, so client rules describe the observed product and configuration at notable severity. Generic endpoint paths and method names do not by themselves establish C2, execution, or a trojanized installer; those combinations are named and graded as clues. References were updated across all stages.
